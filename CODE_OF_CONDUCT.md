# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Give specific, constructive technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Respect privacy, security reports, and project boundaries.
- Accept correction and repair harm when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, vulnerabilities, or confidential reports.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review,
project events, and public spaces where a person represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@cometapi.com`. Do not post
sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code.
Reports will be handled as privately and consistently as the available project
infrastructure permits. Retaliation is prohibited.

## Enforcement guidelines

Responses should be proportional to impact and recurrence:

1. **Correction:** private clarification and a request to change behavior.
2. **Warning:** a documented warning with consequences for recurrence.
3. **Temporary restriction:** time-limited removal from project interaction.
4. **Permanent restriction:** removal for sustained, severe, or dangerous
   behavior.

CometAPI maintainers administer enforcement reports received through
`support@cometapi.com`.
